# 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

**Project:** Netflix Content Analytics  
**Dataset Period:** 2008 – 2021  
**Total Titles Analyzed:** 8,790

---

## 1. Content Type Distribution

| Type | Count | Percentage |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Movies | 6,126 | **69.7%** |
| TV Shows | 2,664 | **30.3%** |

> 📌 **Insight:** Netflix is predominantly a movie platform, with nearly 7 in 10 titles being films. However, the TV Show ratio has been increasing year over year as Netflix invests more in original series.

---

---

## 3. Content Growth Over Time

| Period | Titles Added | YoY Change |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| 2008–2015 | 138 | Baseline |
| 2016 | 426 | +419% |
| 2017 | 1,185 | +178% |
| 2018 | 1,648 | +39% |
| 2019 | **2,016** | +22% (Peak) |
| 2020 | 1,879 | -6.8% (COVID impact) |
| 2021 | 1,498 | -20% (data partial) |

> 📌 **Insight:** Netflix experienced explosive content growth between 2016–2019. The slight dip in 2020 is attributed to COVID-19 production delays. 2021 data is partial (data collected mid-year).

---

## 4. Ratings Analysis

| Rating | Count | Audience |
|--------|-------|---------|
| TV-MA | 3,205 | Mature (17+) |
| TV-14 | 2,157 | Teen (14+) |
| TV-PG | 861 | Family |
| R | 799 | Restricted |
| PG-13 | 490 | Teen+ |

> 📌 **Insight:** Over **60% of Netflix content** is rated TV-MA or TV-14, confirming Netflix's primary target audience is adults. Family-friendly content (TV-Y, TV-Y7, G, PG) makes up only ~12% of the catalog.

## 6. Duration Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Average Movie Duration | **99.6 minutes** |
| Shortest Movie | 3 minutes |
| Longest Movie | 312 minutes |
| TV Shows with 1 Season | ~73% |
| TV Shows with 2+ Seasons | ~27% |

> 📌 **Insight:** Most Netflix original TV shows don't get renewed beyond 1-2 seasons. The average movie length (~100 min) aligns with the standard theatrical film runtime.

---

## 7. Seasonal Patterns

**Peak content addition months:**
1. **December** (812 titles) — Holiday season push
2. **July** (827 titles) — Summer catalog refresh
3. **October** (760 titles) — Halloween content

> 📌 **Insight:** Netflix strategically adds content around major holidays and seasonal events to retain subscribers.

---

## Summary & Recommendations

1. **Netflix is a movie-first platform** but trending toward TV Show investment
2. **Geographic diversity is a core strategy** — US + India alone = 49% of catalog
3. **Mature content dominates** — family-friendly content is underrepresented
4. **Drama & International content** are the backbone genres
5. **Content pipeline peaked in 2019** — COVID disrupted 2020-2021 production
6. **December & July** are the best months to expect new content drops
